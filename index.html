Laws & Community Rules

  1. Follow the Minecraft server's rules.
  2. Respect other citizens and players.
  3. Do not steal from or grief fellow citizens.
  4. Ask before changing or taking shared national property.
  5. Keep public builds and shared areas in good condition.
  6. Follow official decisions made under Debloxia's agreed government system.

How to Join Debloxia

  1. Contact a Debloxia leader or government member in-game or through the community's official chat.
  2. Ask to apply for citizenship and read the current nation rules.
  3. Wait for an approval from the people authorized to accept members.
  4. Once accepted, follow the instructions for joining the settlement and getting started.
